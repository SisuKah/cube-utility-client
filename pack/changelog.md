# Changelog

Patch notes, newest first. The newest section is what players see as "What's new" after the
next release. `npm run pack:add` adds a section for you.

## Full frame rate, even when you're idle

- Minecraft no longer drops to 10 frames a second when you leave it alone for five minutes: Dynamic FPS's Idle Timeout is now Disabled.
- It keeps its full frame rate behind other windows too, and Minecraft's own Inactivity FPS Limit is on Minimized, so it never slows down while you're away from the keyboard.

## Cube Utility 1.8.0, one settings page, Get more and 16 more mods

- New: **Get more**, at the top of the settings page. Keystrokes, TNT timer, Sprint status, Effect timers, Faster inventory, Food info and a tidier F3 screen are already installed: press Get and one is on straight away, with no download and no restart, and gets its own tab with its settings. Remove puts it away again.
- Press Right Shift (or Mods on the title screen) for one settings page with every mod: pick a mod on the left, change its settings on the right. Video, Fullscreen, Eggwars, Background frame rate, Hidden mobs, Hidden blocks, Extra speed-ups and Server features all look alike, with switches, sliders and choices that save as soon as you change them.
- Cube Utility 1.8.0 has zoom, freelook, fullbright, an armour HUD, ping numbers and more chat history built in, so Zoomify, Freelook, More Chat History and Armor Dura HUD left the pack.
- Freelook: hold the middle mouse button to look around. The launcher puts it there for you, unless something else of yours is on that button; then pick a key for "Freelook (look around)" in Controls.
- New: Spectator Inside, KB Delay Meter, Packet Logger, Assistant Bridge, Text Layout Cache, BadOptimizations, MoreCulling, Fast IP Ping, Noxesium and Server Resource Pack Checker. Variants CIT is now on unless you switch it off.
- New players start with tuned settings for every mod.

## Cube Utility Client 1.0.0+mc26.3

- New look in the game itself: a space title screen with a slowly turning galaxy, the client's teal and gold in every menu, and a dark game window with the client's name and icon.

## Cube Utility 1.7.0 and 24 more mods

- Cube Utility 1.7.0, Eggwars Optimizer 1.1.0 and Armor Dura HUD 1.0.0.
- CubeCraft AutoVote and Level + Rank, always on their newest release.
- Sodium, Lithium and the rest of the Eggwars Optimized performance set.
- Optional extras you can switch on or off: Zoomify, More Chat History, Cubes Without Borders, Freelook and Variants CIT.
